# Company Policy — Remote Work

All full-time employees may work remotely up to three days per week.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local timezone.

Employees must submit remote work requests through the HR portal at least 48 hours
in advance. Managers may approve or deny requests based on team coverage needs.

Annual security training is mandatory for all staff and must be completed by March 31.
