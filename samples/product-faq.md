# Product FAQ — Atlas Analytics

Atlas Analytics is a self-serve BI platform for operations teams.

## Pricing

The Starter plan costs $49 per user per month and includes five dashboards.
The Enterprise plan includes SSO, audit logs, and dedicated support.

## Data Sources

Atlas supports PostgreSQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, and CSV uploads.
CSV uploads are limited to 250 MB per file on Starter plans.

## Support

Starter customers receive email support with a 24-hour response SLA.
Enterprise customers receive 24/7 chat and phone support.
